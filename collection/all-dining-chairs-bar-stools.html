Dining Chairs and Bar Stools for Tables, Kitchen Islands and Bar Area

Chester’s brings together classic upholstered seating and contemporary profiles, built to order in our Bangalore workshop. The collection includes the Crown Dining Chair, Raina Dining Chair, and Montel Bar Stool, each designed with considered proportions, solid timber frames, and hand-tailored details. Available in full-grain leather or selected upholstery fabrics, our seating is crafted for residential dining rooms, kitchen islands, and commercial hospitality interiors. Explore our handcrafted range of dining chairs and bar stools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Start by considering the height and size of your dining table or kitchen counter, along with the space available for seating and movement. Dining chairs should allow comfortable legroom and enough space to sit and get up easily, while bar stools should suit the counter height and provide adequate foot support. If you plan to use the seating for extended periods, consider designs with supportive backrests and well-cushioned seats. Chester’s team can help you select suitable designs based on your space, comfort preferences and intended use.

Yes. Our dining chairs and bar stools can be upholstered in full-grain leather or selected fabrics. We offer four signature full-grain leather finishes: Shadow, Nappa, Ready Russet, and Vintage. For fabric upholstery, choices range from 100% French Twill cotton to curated textiles or custom fabrics, depending on the model.

Yes, customisation is possible for many Chester’s dining chairs and bar stools, depending on the design. Upholstery and timber finishes can be discussed with our team, while changes to dimensions depend on the model's construction. We can advise which adjustments are suitable for your chosen chair or stool.

We use 100% full-grain leather across our seating range, available in four signature finishes: Shadow, Nappa, Ready Russet, and Vintage. Sourced from Western Europe and processed at our Tamil Nadu facility, each hide retains its natural grain and texture. We also offer classic leather dining chairs featuring traditional deep buttoning and hand-finished detailing.

Place your leather seating in well-ventilated areas away from damp walls and direct sun. Maintain the finish by dusting regularly with a microfibre cloth and clearing spills quickly. When the surface needs conditioning, simply apply Chester’s leather wax with a clean cloth to restore its natural lustre.

Selected dining chairs and bar stools may be available directly from our Bangalore showroom.
