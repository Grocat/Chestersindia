Benches and Ottomans for Entryways, Bedrooms and Living Spaces

Benches lend a room a longer, more defined line, while ottomans offer a more compact form for seating, resting the feet or serving as an alternative to a coffee table.

Chester's collection combines classic forms with contemporary profiles. Upholstery is available in full-grain leather or selected fabrics, thoughtfully paired with refined timber details. The Coventry Bench features deep buttoning and carefully worked upholstery, while the Queen Anne Ottoman is distinguished by its cabriole legs. Made by skilled artisans in our Bangalore workshops, these pieces reflect Chester’s attention to proportion, material and finish.

Explore our handcrafted leather ottomans, including the classic Chesterfield leather ottoman, or discover a deep-buttoned leather bench.

Chester’s benches and ottomans are upholstered in full-grain leather, available in four finishes: Nappa, Shadow, Vintage and Ready Russet.
