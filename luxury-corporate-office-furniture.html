Luxury Modern Office Furniture for Distinctive Corporate Interiors

Executive seating and upholstered furniture for CEO cabins, boardrooms and reception lounges, built in our own Bangalore workshops.

Discuss Your Corporate Project

At Chester's India, we make executive seating and upholstered furniture for premium corporate environments across India. Our work lives in CEO cabins, boardrooms, reception lounges and the client-facing spaces where an organisation is at its most deliberate.

Since 1986, we have furnished spaces meant to carry presence. Our luxury office furniture in India comes down to proportion, presence in the room, and comfort that holds up over years. Built for daily use, our pieces are made to mature over decades, not seasons.

We work with corporate clients, architects and interior designers to select or develop furniture suited to the architecture, daily use and visual language of each space.

Seating

Seating with Presence and Engineering

Our executive swivel chairs draw inspiration from Regency forms and clean modern silhouettes. The range combines ergonomic support with considered engineering. Ergonomics and mechanical precision are prioritized alongside visual weight, incorporating imported Donati 360° tilt and swivel mechanisms for smooth, reliable movement.

The collection includes the Executive Chesterfield, Hybrid, President, Heritage, Boss, Prima and Highbury chairs, offering options for different desks, proportions and working preferences.

For a leadership suite, a chair is rarely the whole answer. For clients seeking a CEO office leather sofa in India, Chester's offers Chesterfield and modern designs suited to leadership cabins, reception lounges and private meeting areas.

Craft

Made in Bangalore, Considered in Every Detail

Every piece of our handcrafted furniture in Bangalore is produced in our dedicated Peenya workshops. Frames combine solid steam beech and Canadian pine, while visible timber sections use Burma teak. Hides are sourced from Central and Western Europe. Because woodworking, upholstery, and finishing remain under one roof, we can hold consistent quality across all upholstery and leather finishes while supporting future repair and refurbishment.

Bespoke

Developed Around the Interior and Its Purpose

Architects and designers specifying luxury corporate interiors furniture in Bangalore can share floor plans, design intent, quantities, and timelines, and we will build to the brief.

From Design Brief to Finished Furniture

Each project follows a considered process from the initial dialogue to hand led reviews, delivery, and ongoing care.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What furniture can Chester's provide for a CEO office?
Chester's supplies executive swivel chairs, leather sofas, accent armchairs, and ottomans. Seating recommendations are tailored to room dimensions, functional needs, and the overall interior aesthetic.
Can office furniture be made in custom dimensions?
Yes. Selected designs can be modified in scale, depth, and proportion through bespoke furniture in Bangalore, provided structural integrity and ergonomic balance are maintained.
Which leather is suitable for executive office furniture?
Selection depends on preference: Shadow leather offers hand dyed depth, Nappa provides a smooth, contemporary feel, and Vintage finishes deliver a classic, antiqued patina. Each develops a different character over time, allowing the choice to reflect both the project and the client's preferences.
Does Chester's work with architects and corporate interior designers?
Yes. Chester's collaborates directly with architects, interior designers, and corporate trade teams on private cabin, boardroom, and institutional projects.
