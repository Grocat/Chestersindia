Handcrafted Luxury Furniture for Interiors and Architectural Projects

A considered interior depends on furniture that belongs to the space as naturally as its lighting, materials, and architecture. Chester’s India works with architects and interior designers to develop loose furniture around those requirements, from upholstery and timber finishes to proportions and detailing. With a heritage in leather furniture and in-house production in Bangalore, the workshop creates handcrafted luxury furniture for interiors across residential, hospitality, and corporate projects.

A Furniture Partner for Considered Interiors

A successful room relies on a unified design language. Specification often begins with the scale of the room, a spatial plan, or a raw material palette rather than an isolated retail product. Chester’s India works as a furniture partner, helping design practices move from initial mood boards and layouts to approved dimensions, upholstery, timber finishes, and detailing. In-house design development and production allow each piece to feel considered within the wider interior scheme.

Furniture for the Spaces You Are Shaping

We focus on loose and upholstered furniture, providing design practices with adaptable pieces for distinct interior sectors.

Private Residences

For living rooms, libraries, private studies, bedrooms, dining spaces, and lounges, designers can begin with Chesterfields, Classics, French Club Chairs, or Modern pieces as considered visual anchors.

Hospitality and Social Spaces

For hotel lobbies, bars, restaurants, guest suites, clubs, and private lounges, seating must bring visual character together with the construction required for regular use. Chester’s develops luxury hospitality furniture in Bangalore around the proportions, materials, and atmosphere of each space.

Corporate and Executive Interiors

For reception areas, executive cabins, boardrooms, law offices, private meeting rooms, and business lounges, luxury corporate interiors furniture can introduce a sense of permanence without overwhelming the architecture.

Made with the Detail a Project Demands

Chester’s produces its upholstered furniture within its dedicated workshops in Peenya, Bangalore. Concealed frames use a carefully considered combination of solid steam beech and Canadian pine, while visible timber elements are made from Burma teak. Hides sourced from Central and Western Europe are processed through Chester’s production system before upholstery begins.
Deep buttoning is completed through a precise, hand-driven process, with every button secured using waxed, high-strength yarn that passes through the frame. Each seam is sewn using reinforced lock-stitching at 8–10 stitches per inch, with tension calibrated to accommodate the natural movement of leather. These documented processes distinguish Chester’s among established luxury sofa manufacturers.

Furniture Enquiries for Designers and Architects

Can an existing Chester’s design be adapted for a project?
Yes. Designers can discuss changes to dimensions, proportions, upholstery, timber stains, and detailing based on the selected design and project requirements.
What information should be shared when sourcing furniture for interior designers and architects?
Share the project type, location, intended spaces, approximate quantities, preferred design direction, drawings or mood boards, and expected schedule. This helps the team understand the project before recommending suitable furniture for interior designers and architects.
Can designers review leather and finish options?
Yes. Designers can review the available leather families, fabrics, caning, timber stains, and detailing on the Upholstery and Finishes page. Project teams can also contact Chester’s to discuss samples and material requirements.
Where is Chester’s furniture made?
Chester’s furniture is produced within its dedicated workshops in Peenya, Bangalore, where frame construction, upholstery, deep buttoning, stitching, and finishing are undertaken.
Does Chester’s provide furniture restoration or refurbishment?
Chester’s accepts enquiries concerning the restoration or repair of older Chester’s furniture. The team can assess the condition of the piece and advise whether suitable work can be undertaken.
