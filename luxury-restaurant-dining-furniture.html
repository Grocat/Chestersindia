Luxury Restaurant Furniture in Bangalore

A dining space requires a careful spatial balance where seating must support the culinary narrative while enduring continuous service. Chester’s India operates as a dedicated manufacturing partner for luxury hospitality interiors, providing custom seating for fine-dining rooms, boutique hotels, cafés, cocktail bars, and private clubs. The portfolio spans handcrafted dining chairs, bar stools, lounge chairs, sofas, benches, and ottomans. Selected designs can be adapted through upholstery, leather selection, timber stains, proportions, and detailing, with guest comfort, material character, and proportion considered from the outset.

Founded in Bangalore in 1986, Chester’s brings four decades of in-house furniture-making experience to commercial and hospitality projects.

Discuss Your Restaurant Project

Furniture That Shapes the Dining Experience

Guests experience a room physically as much as visually. The density of a cushion, the incline of a backrest, and the tactile quality of full-grain leather all shape the experience of comfort from the moment guests are seated. Together, these details allow the seating to work in harmony with the architectural lighting, layout, and atmosphere. Chester’s operates as a furniture project partner rather than a simple supplier, collaborating with architects and interior designers to ensure every piece strengthens the interior architecture.

Seating for Every Part of the Restaurant

Hospitality layouts require specialised loose and upholstered furniture categories across distinct service zones:

Dining Chairs and Dining Armchairs:

Formal dining rooms require seating that considers proportion, comfort, and its relationship with the surrounding interior. Chester’s premium dining chairs for restaurants can be adapted through upholstery, timber finishes, and detailing to suit the character of the space.

Restaurant and Bar Furniture:

Chester’s offers hand-stitched leather and fabric dining chairs, bar stools, and compact seating for cocktail bars and private dining rooms, with materials and finishes selected to complement the wider interior.

Hotel Lobby and Lounge Furniture:

For hotel lobbies and lounge areas, Chester’s offers deep-buttoned Chesterfields, leather lounge chairs, French club chairs, upholstered benches, and ottomans, with designs adapted to project requirements. For larger reception areas, handcrafted luxury sofas can anchor the layout while maintaining a considered connection with the surrounding interior.

Private Clubs and Executive Spaces:

Formal hospitality areas call for furniture with a sense of permanence. Chester’s offers library chairs, executive swivel chairs, and structured leather sofas for boardrooms, cigar lounges, and private members' clubs. Bespoke dining chairs for hotels can also be developed to carry a consistent design language across private dining rooms and adjoining hospitality spaces.

Designed Around the Way Guests Use the Space

Seating layouts must adapt to specific operational formats rather than relying on a single uniform style.

Fine-Dining Restaurants:

In fine-dining settings, guests may remain at the table for several hours, making comfort an important part of the experience. Premium seating for fine dining restaurants combines supportive forms with carefully selected materials, allowing the furniture to complement the atmosphere without drawing attention away from the dining experience.

Contemporary Restaurants and Cafés:

Active dining spaces require flexible configurations. Compact tub chairs and light bar stools optimise spatial flow, making Chester’s relevant to restaurant and café furniture projects in Bangalore.

Bars and Social Lounges:

Intimate cocktail lounges depend on low lighting and deep, protective seating. Classic club chairs, low-slung sofas, and upholstered ottomans establish an inviting atmosphere.

Hotels and Private Clubs:

Multi-use properties require versatile furniture. Sourcing custom furniture for luxury hotels allows seating to be developed with the proportions, materials, and construction required for frequently used dining rooms, libraries, and lounges.

Made to Belong Within the Interior

Selecting furniture requires balancing a restaurant's architecture, floor plan, and table arrangement. Designers can use existing Chester’s designs as a foundation, customising chair width, seat depth, arm height, and table clearance to protect server movement, so handcrafted luxury furniture for interiors feels considered as part of the property’s architecture.

Materials Chosen to Support the Dining Atmosphere

Surface materials should sit comfortably with a room's lighting, colour palette and flooring. The workshop offers a range of fabrics, timber stains and woven caning alongside its leathers. Shadow leather, hand-dyed one piece at a time, is well suited to fine-dining rooms, developing a rich patina with regular use. For restaurant furniture in Bangalore, choosing materials that gain character with age means the room looks better in its fifth year than its first.

Made with Care for Comfort and Continued Use

Commercial environments place furniture under the demands of regular use. Concealed frames use a considered combination of solid steam beech and Canadian pine, while visible timber elements are made from teak.

Working with experienced restaurant furniture manufacturers in Bangalore helps balance distinctive visual character with the construction standards required for hospitality environments. For architects and designers looking for luxury restaurant furniture in Bangalore, every detail matters, from the feel of the materials to the proportions of a chair within the room. Chester’s combines carefully selected materials with considered craftsmanship to create furniture that brings character, comfort and lasting presence to hospitality interiors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Chester’s customise dining chairs for a restaurant?
Yes. Frame dimensions, seat depths, arm heights, timber stains, and upholstery materials can be adjusted to suit your restaurant layout and seating requirements.
What types of restaurant furniture does Chester’s make?
Chester’s makes loose restaurant furniture including dining chairs, armchairs, bar stools, club chairs, sofas, benches, and ottomans. The range is designed as loose furniture rather than fitted joinery, allowing individual pieces to be specified according to the requirements of the space.
How long does a restaurant furniture order take?
We confirm your timeline when the order is placed, because it depends on the pieces, the finishes and the workshop's schedule at that point. For a restaurant or hotel opening, tell us your handover date at the consultation and we will plan production and delivery around it.
Where is Chester’s restaurant furniture made?
All of our production takes place within our own workshops in Peenya, Bangalore, where woodworking, upholstery and finishing happen under one roof.
How does Chester’s leather age, and what care does it need over time?
Chester’s leather is made for everyday use and can retain its character and durability for years with proper care. Over time, natural leather develops subtle changes in colour, texture, and softness as it is handled and exposed to light. Shadow and Vintage leathers are designed to develop a deeper patina with use, while Nappa retains its softer feel with gentler care. Dust regularly with a dry cloth, wipe spills promptly, and follow the care guidance for the specific leather finish.
Can restaurant furniture be restored or reupholstered?
Yes. Chester’s offers frame repairs, leather restoration, and reupholstery for suitable Chester’s pieces. The furniture is assessed before restoration to determine the work required and the most appropriate materials and treatment.
Does Chester’s India offer a warranty on restaurant furniture?
Yes. Every frame built by Chester’s carries a 10-year guarantee. Frames use solid steam beech and Canadian pine, with the timber selected according to the requirements of each part of the frame. Specific warranty terms and coverage are confirmed at the time of order.
Where can I experience Chester’s restaurant furniture and leather collection in person?
You can experience Chester’s restaurant furniture and leather collection at our shop on Infantry Road in Bengaluru, open Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 7 pm. You can examine the furniture, compare leather hides, and see how finishes such as Shadow respond to light before making a selection.
