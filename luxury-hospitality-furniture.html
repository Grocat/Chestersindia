Luxury Hospitality Furniture in Bangalore

Chester’s India manufactures handcrafted leather seating and tailored loose furniture for luxury hotels, boutique properties, private clubs, and fine dining establishments. Produced within Chester’s dedicated workshops in Bangalore, the furniture can be specified in selected dimensions, upholstery and finish options to suit the requirements of each hospitality setting. This furniture is specifically developed for high visibility interiors where physical comfort, architectural character, and long term structural performance must remain consistently aligned. The aim is furniture that remains comfortable, serviceable and relevant through years of continued use.

Furniture That Defines the Guest Experience

In high end hospitality, furniture creates the initial tactile connection between a guest and the property. For luxury hospitality furniture in Bangalore, Chester’s approaches luxury hotel furniture in India through seating that feels distinct, comfortable and naturally connected to the surrounding architecture.

Chester’s origins date to 1986, with its specialist furniture making expertise strengthened in 1994 when four experienced craftsmen trained in the Netherlands in deep buttoned leather furniture. As a luxury furniture supplier for hospitality projects, Chester’s works directly with hoteliers, architects, interior designers and trade teams to develop loose furniture around the requirements of the property.

Crafted for Character, Comfort and Continued Use

Behind every piece placed in a guest area lies a manufacturing process engineered for endurance.

We were among the first workshops in India and an established hospitality furniture manufacturer in Bangalore, to take up the centuries old Chesterfield technique. Chester's brings four decades of specialised furniture making experience to luxury hotel furniture projects in Bangalore and across India. Each Chester’s piece moves through a five stage process covering framework construction, upholstery, finishing, quality review and post purchase care.

Solid timber frames provide structural support for continued use, while hides are sourced from Central and Western Europe and processed through Chester’s tanneries in Tamil Nadu. Selected finishes, including Shadow leather, receive additional hand dyeing.

Upholstery tension, deep buttoning, and timber shaping are executed entirely in house. This direct oversight allows hoteliers, architects, and project teams to rely on long term maintenance, restoration, and refurbishment rather than routine product replacement.

Translating these manufacturing capabilities into a specific property requires a tailored approach to scale and material.

Made Around the Property, Not Added Afterwards

Distinctive hospitality interiors sometimes require furniture beyond standard catalogue dimensions or finishes. Chester’s provides custom furniture for luxury hotels, adjusting established designs or developing project specific pieces to match precise interior layouts.

Depending on the design, project teams can discuss changes to dimensions, proportions, upholstery, leather and timber finishes, and selected detailing. Whether furnishing a boutique heritage property, an urban luxury hotel, or an exclusive private club, custom pieces ensure that the furniture reflects the scale, colour palette, and operational demands of the space.

Materials Chosen to Age Beautifully

Every material is selected to acquire character through constant handling rather than deteriorating with age. Internal frames are engineered using solid steam beech and Canadian pine for structural longevity, while exposed wood elements feature fine Burma teak.

Leathers are selected for their ability to develop a rich, natural patina over time. Upholstery techniques feature waxed high strength yarn anchored through the frame and tight lock stitching at 8–10 stitches per inch. These material choices allow the furniture to withstand high traffic hospitality environments while growing more visually compelling over years of use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Chester’s make custom furniture for luxury hotels?
Yes. We work to architectural specification, either adapting an existing design or developing a new one, with dimensions and leather finishes set by the project. Most of what we send to hotels has been changed in some way.
What types of hospitality spaces can Chester’s furnish?
Chester’s supplies loose seating for hotel lobbies, guest suites, executive lounges, fine dining restaurants, bars, private clubs, and boutique commercial properties.
Can custom dimensions and finishes be requested?
Yes frame dimensions, seating depth, cushion firmness, wood stain, and leather finishes can all be adjusted through our trade programme. See our custom dimensions and finishes page for the available options.
Can architects and designers request samples or technical information?
Yes. Architects and interior designers can request leather swatch books, timber finish samples, and dimensional specifications directly from the trade team for project planning.
Where is Chester’s furniture manufactured?
All woodworking, frame assembly, and upholstery take place within our Peenya workshops, with leather sourced and tanned in our own facilities before final hand-finishing in-house.
Does Chester’s provide restoration or refurbishment?
Yes. Chester’s offers full restoration services, including leather re-dyeing, frame re-springing, and complete reupholstery, allowing properties to maintain their furniture long-term.
Does Chester’s supply furniture outside Bangalore?
Yes. Chester’s manufactures in Bangalore and delivers completed furniture to hospitality projects across India, coordinating transport and site logistics with project managers.
